# Problem 11: Power & Energy

**Question:** What is the power dissipated by a 100 Ω resistor when a voltage of 50 V is applied across it? How much energy is consumed in 5 minutes?

---

### The Intuition
Imagine rubbing your hands together really fast. **Voltage** is how hard you press them together, and **Resistance** is the friction. **Power** is how intensely your hands heat up in that exact moment. **Energy**, on the other hand, is the total amount of heat you generated over the entire 5 minutes. Power is the *rate*, Energy is the *total accumulation*.

### Metrics
* **Resistance ($R$):** The opposition to current flow, measured in Ohms ($\Omega$).
* **Voltage ($V$):** The electrical pressure pushing the charge, measured in Volts (V).
* **Power ($P$):** The rate at which electrical energy is converted to heat (dissipated), measured in Watts (W).
* **Time ($t$):** Duration of the energy use, measured in seconds (s).
* **Energy ($E$):** The total work done or heat generated over time, measured in Joules (J).

### Solution

**1. Calculate Power**
The formula linking Power, Voltage, and Resistance is:
$$P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Plug in the given values:
$$P = \frac{50^2}{100}$$
$$P = \frac{2500}{100}$$
$$P = 25 \text{ W}$$

**2. Calculate Energy Consumed**
Energy is Power multiplied by Time. First, convert 5 minutes to seconds:
$$t = 5 \times 60 = 300 \text{ s}$$

Now, multiply Power by Time:
$$E = P \times t$$
$$E = 25 \times 300$$
$$E = 7,500 \text{ J}$$

**Final Answers:**
* The power dissipated is **25 W** (Watts).
* The total energy consumed is **7,500 J** (Joules).
